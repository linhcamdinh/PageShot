# Pageshot privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Pageshot is a Chrome extension for capturing, annotating and saving screenshots. This policy explains what information Pageshot handles.

## Screenshots stay on your computer

Screenshots you take with Pageshot, and any annotations you add, are processed entirely inside your browser. They are stored temporarily in the extension's local storage so the editor can open them, and they are saved only to the location you choose. Pageshot never uploads, transmits or shares your screenshots or the content of the pages you capture.

## Information stored on your device

Pageshot stores the following in Chrome's extension storage:

- Your editor preferences (colors, sizes, fonts) and save preferences (format, quality, folder)
- The date your free trial started
- Whether you have purchased Pageshot, and when that was last checked
- Your 20 most recent captures, so the editor can reopen them. Older captures are deleted automatically

If Chrome sync is on, the trial start date and the purchase key may sync across your Chrome browsers. Nothing else is synced.

## Payments

Purchases are processed by [ExtensionPay](https://extensionpay.com), which uses [Stripe](https://stripe.com) for card payments. When you buy Pageshot or restore a purchase, you enter your email address and payment details on ExtensionPay's pages, not inside Pageshot. Pageshot itself never sees or stores your card details. To confirm your purchase, Pageshot sends ExtensionPay a random key that identifies your installation and receives back whether you have paid.

ExtensionPay's handling of your information is described in its own privacy policy on extensionpay.com, and Stripe's at https://stripe.com/privacy.

## What Pageshot does not do

- No analytics, tracking or advertising
- No account or sign up
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties
- No reading of pages you have not chosen to capture

## Permissions

Pageshot asks only for the permissions it needs to capture the tab you choose (activeTab, scripting), capture your screen when you ask (desktopCapture), save files (downloads), remember your settings (storage), and confirm purchases on extensionpay.com.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Pageshot removes all of its local data. To delete your purchase record, contact ExtensionPay.
